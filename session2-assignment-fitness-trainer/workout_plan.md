## Why this plan  
- This 3‑day program is built around **progressive overload with dumbbells**, the only equipment you have at home, while keeping the workouts to **45‑60 min**.  
- It prioritises **upper‑body and core work** (your main goals) and uses **neutral‑grip, elbow‑friendly movements** to protect your tennis elbow and **low‑impact hip‑hinge patterns** to spare the lower back.  
- The structure follows classic beginner guidelines from Arnold Schwarzenegger’s *Encyclopedia of Modern Bodybuilding* and the longevity‑focused principles in Strauss Zelnick’s *Becoming Ageless*.

---

## Weekly Split  

| Day | Focus | Session Length |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| **Day 1 – Upper‑Body Push & Core** | Chest, shoulders, triceps, core | 45‑60 min |
| **Day 2 – Upper‑Body Pull & Core** | Back, biceps (elbow‑friendly), rear delts, core | 45‑60 min |
| **Day 3 – Lower‑Body & Functional Core** | Legs, glutes, posterior chain, core stability | 45‑60 min |
| **Rest** | – | – (any non‑training day) |

*Train on non‑consecutive days (e.g., Mon‑Wed‑Fri) to give muscles and joints recovery time.*

## Day 1 – Upper‑Body Push & Core  

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Form / Intensity Cue |
|----------|------|------|------|----------------------|
| 1. **Dynamic Warm‑up** (arm circles, band pull‑aparts, torso twists) | 1 | 60 sec each | — | Light, full range of motion |
| 2. **Dumbbell Floor Press** (neutral grip) | 3 | 10‑12 | 90 sec | Press from the floor, elbows at ~45°, keep shoulder blades retracted. |
| 3. **Standing Dumbbell Arnold Press** (light weight, neutral grip) | 3 | 10‑12 | 90 sec | Press up and rotate slightly; stop if elbow pain spikes. |
| 4. **Incline Dumbbell Press** (set an adjustable bench to 30‑45°) | 3 | 8‑10 | 90 sec | Keep wrists neutral; avoid locking elbows. |
| 5. **Dumbbell Lateral Raise** (light, neutral grip) | 2 | 12‑15 | 60 sec | Slight bend in elbows, raise to shoulder height only. |
| 6. **Triceps Kick‑Back** (neutral grip) | 2 | 12‑15 | 60 sec | Keep upper arm parallel to floor, squeeze the triceps at the end. |
| 7. **Plank** | 3 | 30‑45 sec | 60 sec | Core tight, hips level, don’t let the lower back sag. |
| 8. **Dead‑Bug** (bodyweight) | 2 | 10‑12 per side | 45 sec | Keep lower back pressed into the floor; move slowly. |
| 9. **Cool‑down stretch** (pec stretch, shoulder stretch) | 1 | 60 sec each | — | Hold gently, no bouncing. |

*Total time ≈ 50 min.*
